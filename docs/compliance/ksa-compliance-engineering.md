# KSA compliance engineering (methodology)

## Scope of this public document

High-level lifecycle thinking for regulated Saudi operations on an ERP platform. **No proprietary algorithms, endpoints, or credentials.**

## Control themes

- Separate **configuration**, **master data**, **transaction posting**, and **submission/reporting** stages.
- Gate go-live on evidence packages, not slideware.
- Isolate Sandbox experiments from Production authority.
- Treat privacy (PDPL-oriented design) as first-class for HR and personal data.

## Domains typically involved

E-invoicing controls, labor/HR controls, payroll statutory outputs, regulated inventory attributes, and POS continuity—each with its own specialist SoT in a private program.
