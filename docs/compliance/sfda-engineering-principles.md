# SFDA / regulated inventory principles (public)

## Intent

When inventory carries regulated attributes (batches, expiry, controlled handling), ERP design must preserve traceability and prevent silent cross-company or cross-warehouse leakage.

## Principles

- Lot/serial identity is part of the control surface.
- Operational shortcuts that bypass traceability are defects.
- Reporting must not become an uncontrolled exfiltration path.
- Changes require specialist review beyond generic stock PRs.

Implementation details remain private.
