# 08 — KSA compliance engineering

## Problem
Regulatory programs stall when treated as a single “module install” event.

## Observed symptom
Partial Sandbox demos mistaken for Production readiness; missing evidence packs.

## Forensic reasoning
Separate configuration, master data, transactional proving, and submission/reporting readiness.

## Root cause / engineering challenge
Compliance as feature checkbox instead of gated lifecycle.

## Minimal safe solution
Phase gates with specialist SoTs; Sandbox≠Production proof rules; privacy for personal data domains.

## Validation method
Evidence pack completeness reviews; explicit non-claims when Production untouched.

## Engineering lesson
Methodology beats heroics.

## Public-safe evidence
Lifecycle diagram in prose (docs/compliance).

## What remains private
Implementation, credentials, and production endpoints.
